Interpretation Guidance

How to read a verdict in an operational environment.

fronted

SNI/Host divergence on a shared-anycast CDN edge, often with custom TLS stack markers. Treat as a high-priority alert: investigate origin reachability, inspect allow-lists, review routing fabric. OWASP A01/A04/A10 - NIST DE.

suspicious

Two or more alignment anomalies (authority divergence, TTL, fingerprint drift). Correlate across time windows and peers before escalating. OWASP A08/A09 - NIST DE/RS.

benign

Consistent SNI/Host/cert/route. Note that absence of evidence is not evidence of absence - ECH and full CDN-legit fronting stay invisible to passive inspection.
